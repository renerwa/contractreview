ContractReview AI now supports a structured review result experience for professional contract review workflows.

## What changed

- Overall risk summary with high, medium, and low risk grouping.
- Clause-level findings with original text, issue explanation, and suggested revision.
- Review context preserved in the report, including perspective, signing country, output language, and focus areas.
- Print-ready report view for sharing with legal and business stakeholders.

This release turns the workflow from document preparation into a complete AI contract review report.
