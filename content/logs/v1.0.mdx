## Highlights

- Upload or paste contract text from the landing page.
- Parse contract content and prepare a pre-analysis summary.
- Identify when a document does not look like a contract.
- Configure review perspective, signing country, output language, and focus areas.

This release establishes the main workflow from contract intake to review setup.
