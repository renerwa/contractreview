AI contract review applies large language models to contract documents so legal and business teams can find risk faster and review clauses with a more consistent structure.

It is not just contract summarization. A useful review workflow should connect each finding to the contract text, explain why the language may create risk, and suggest a revision path that a reviewer can evaluate.

## What AI contract review should analyze

Effective AI contract review should cover the core information that affects business risk:

- The contract type and review perspective
- The signing country or jurisdiction context
- Commercial obligations, payment terms, renewals, and termination rights
- Liability, indemnity, confidentiality, data protection, and IP ownership
- Clause-level evidence and practical suggested revisions

## Why teams use it

Legal, procurement, sales, and business teams often review many similar agreements under time pressure. AI contract review helps them triage contracts, surface unusual or one-sided clauses, and prepare negotiation points before a human reviewer makes the final decision.

## What a professional output looks like

A professional AI contract review report should include an overall risk level, ranked risk findings, original clause references, risk explanations, and suggested revisions. It should also preserve the review context, including the review side, signing country, output language, and custom focus areas.
