## Introduction

Welcome to ContractReview AI. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information when you use our AI contract review service.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Name, email address, authentication information, and account settings.
   - **Purpose**: To create and manage your account, provide support, and secure access.

2. **Contract and Review Data**
   - **What We Collect**: Contract files, pasted contract text, extracted clauses, review settings, risk findings, and generated reports.
   - **Purpose**: To parse contracts, run pre-analysis, generate review results, and preserve your review workspace.

3. **Usage Details**
   - **What We Collect**: Product interactions, feature usage, review status, and system events.
   - **Purpose**: To improve reliability, workflow quality, and user experience.

4. **Device and Log Data**
   - **What We Collect**: Browser type, device information, IP-related technical logs, and error logs.
   - **Purpose**: To maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and prevent abuse.

5. **Payment and Billing Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Billing records and payment status handled through payment providers.
   - **Purpose**: To process purchases, subscriptions, invoices, and account credits.

## Contract Data Security

Contracts often contain sensitive business information. We apply access controls, secure storage practices, and operational safeguards to protect uploaded documents and review outputs. Contract data is used to provide the review workflow and is not used to train public AI models.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or contract data. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate authentication, storage, payments, analytics, email, or AI processing, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect user safety, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to enforce our terms.

## Data Retention

We retain account, contract, and review data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. You may request deletion of eligible data by contacting support.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes become effective when posted on this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at [support@contractreview.ai](mailto:support@contractreview.ai).
