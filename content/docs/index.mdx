## What is ContractReview AI?

ContractReview AI is an AI contract review platform for legal, procurement, sales, and business teams. It helps users upload or paste contracts, extract key context, identify risk levels, and generate structured revision suggestions.

## Review workflow

1. Upload a PDF, Word, or text contract, or paste contract text directly.
2. Let AI parse the document and prepare a pre-analysis summary.
3. Confirm the review perspective, signing country, output language, and focus areas.
4. Start the full review to generate risk findings, clause evidence, explanations, and suggested revisions.
5. Export or print the final review report for internal review and negotiation.

## What the result includes

The review report includes an overall risk judgment, high/medium/low risk grouping, original clause references, issue explanations, suggested revisions, and the review settings used for the run.
